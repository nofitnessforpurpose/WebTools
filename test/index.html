Quick-Start Guide

What is NFfP:LinkTool?

NFfP:LinkTool is an offline browser utility for transferring Datapaks to and from the vintage Psion Organiser II via the Comms Link interface (RS-232, USB Serial, or Bluetooth RFCOMM).

It implements the Psion Link bootstrap injection protocol to stream raw packs with hardware CRC-16 integrity verification.

Step-by-Step Procedure
1
Connect Hardware
Insert the Comms Link into Slot D (top peripheral). Insert your source or target pack into Slot B: or Slot C:. Connect serial cable or Bluetooth RFCOMM adapter to your computer or phone.
2
Prepare Psion Organiser II
Turn ON the Psion. Navigate to: COMMS -> BOOT.
At the NAME: prompt, leave it completely blank. Do NOT press EXE yet.
3
Configure in Browser
Choose Read (dump pack to PC/phone) or Write (flash .OPK to pack). Select target slot (Slot B: or Slot C:). Click Connect and pick your serial/Bluetooth port.
4
Start Transfer
Press EXE on the Psion Organiser II. The tool automatically injects the machine-code driver and transfers the pack with real-time percentage progress.
Slot Architecture & Vpp Rules

Drive A: (Internal RAM): Direct memory streaming via internal registers.

Slots B: & C: (External Side Slots): Feature 21V $V_{pp}$ lines. Support RamPacks, Flashpaks, and UV EPROMs.

Slot D: (Top Peripheral): Occupied by Comms Link. Datapak chip selects are arbitrated over Port 6 between packet bursts.
