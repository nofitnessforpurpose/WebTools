Quick-Start Guide

What is COMMSParam?

COMMSParam is a visual configuration workbench and frame simulator for serial communications on the Psion Organiser II (CM, XP, LZ, LZ64, POS series).

It calculates, formats, and decompiles the 15-parameter LSET: OPL statement and verifies physical RS-232 cable pinout wiring.

4-Step Quick Workflow
1
Select a Hardware Preset
Use the Preset dropdown at the top to instantly load verified configurations for PC COM ports, modems, dot-matrix printers, HP plotters, or vintage microcomputers.
2
Tune Parameters in Card 2
Choose your Baud Rate (P1) (color-coded by use type: Cyan for PC/Link, Amber for Modems, Purple for Vintage TTY), Parity (P2), Data Bits (P3), and Flow Control (P5).
3
Verify Cable Wiring in Card 4
Check the live interactive schematic to see which pins connect between your Psion Comms Link and target device. Glowing lines show live signals.
4
Copy Code or LSET Command
Copy the one-line LSET:(...) from Card 1 into your OPL procedure, or grab complete sample code from Card 6 (Code Studio) in OPL, Python, or QBASIC.
Hardware & Wiring Fundamentals

Psion is DCE (Data Communications Equipment):
The Psion Comms Link presents a DB-25 Female Socket. Connecting to an IBM PC (DTE) uses a direct 1:1 cable. Connecting to a Modem (DCE) requires a crossover null-modem adapter.

Handshake (P5) Modes:
0 = None (3-Wire) 1 = XON/XOFF 2 = RTS/CTS 4 = DTR Printer

Interactive Features
  • Bidirectional Decompiler: Paste any LSET:(...) code into Card 1 and click Decompile to reverse-engineer its settings.
  • Bit-Level Waveform: Click bit boxes in Card 3 to flip logic bits ($0 \leftrightarrow 1$) and inspect RS-232 bipolar line voltages ($-12\text{V} \leftrightarrow +12\text{V}$).
  • Live Serial Transmitter: Test sending characters or strings in Card 7 to verify frame timing and throughput.
Pro Tip

You can collapse or expand any card by clicking its title bar to tailor your workspace. Press Esc anytime to close this Guide panel.
